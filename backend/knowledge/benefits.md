# Benefits & Tunjangan

## Health Insurance — BPJS Kesehatan (English)
All employees are enrolled in BPJS Kesehatan (national health insurance) from their first day. The monthly contribution is 5% of salary, split 4% paid by the company and 1% by the employee, and covers the employee, spouse, and up to 3 children.

## Asuransi Kesehatan — BPJS Kesehatan (Bahasa Indonesia)
Seluruh karyawan didaftarkan ke BPJS Kesehatan sejak hari pertama bekerja. Iuran bulanan sebesar 5% dari gaji, dibagi 4% ditanggung perusahaan dan 1% oleh karyawan, mencakup karyawan, pasangan, dan hingga 3 anak.

## Social Security — BPJS Ketenagakerjaan (English)
Employees are covered by BPJS Ketenagakerjaan, which includes work accident insurance (JKK), death benefit (JKM), old-age savings (JHT), and pension (JP). Contributions are shared between the company and the employee as set by regulation.

## Jaminan Sosial — BPJS Ketenagakerjaan (Bahasa Indonesia)
Karyawan dilindungi BPJS Ketenagakerjaan yang mencakup Jaminan Kecelakaan Kerja (JKK), Jaminan Kematian (JKM), Jaminan Hari Tua (JHT), dan Jaminan Pensiun (JP). Iuran ditanggung bersama oleh perusahaan dan karyawan sesuai peraturan.

## Religious Holiday Allowance — THR (English)
Every employee who has worked at least 1 month receives a Religious Holiday Allowance (Tunjangan Hari Raya / THR), paid no later than 7 days before the religious holiday. Employees with 12 or more months of service receive one month's salary; those with less receive a pro-rated amount.

## Tunjangan Hari Raya — THR (Bahasa Indonesia)
Setiap karyawan yang telah bekerja minimal 1 bulan menerima Tunjangan Hari Raya (THR), dibayarkan paling lambat 7 hari sebelum hari raya keagamaan. Karyawan dengan masa kerja 12 bulan atau lebih menerima 1 bulan gaji; yang kurang dari itu menerima secara proporsional.

## Overtime — Lembur (English)
Overtime is voluntary and requires written consent. It is paid at the statutory overtime rates and is limited to a maximum of 4 hours per day and 18 hours per week, per Government Regulation PP 35/2021.

## Lembur (Bahasa Indonesia)
Lembur bersifat sukarela dan memerlukan persetujuan tertulis. Lembur dibayar sesuai tarif upah lembur yang berlaku dan dibatasi maksimal 4 jam per hari dan 18 jam per minggu, sesuai PP 35/2021.

## Remote Work & Allowances (English)
Employees may work from home up to 2 days per week with manager approval. The company provides a monthly internet allowance of Rp 300,000, an annual learning budget of Rp 7,500,000 for courses and conferences, and a monthly wellness stipend of Rp 250,000.

## Kerja Remote & Tunjangan (Bahasa Indonesia)
Karyawan dapat bekerja dari rumah maksimal 2 hari per minggu dengan persetujuan atasan. Perusahaan menyediakan tunjangan internet Rp 300.000 per bulan, anggaran pembelajaran Rp 7.500.000 per tahun untuk kursus dan konferensi, serta tunjangan wellness Rp 250.000 per bulan.
