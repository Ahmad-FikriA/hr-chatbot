# Leave & Cuti Policy | Kebijakan Cuti

## 1. Annual Leave | Cuti Tahunan
Employees who have completed 12 months of continuous service are entitled to 12 working days of paid annual leave per year, in line with Indonesia's Manpower Law (UU Ketenagakerjaan / UU Cipta Kerja). Up to 6 unused days may be carried over to the next year; the rest expires on 31 December. Submit annual leave requests through the HR portal at least 3 working days in advance.
---
Karyawan yang telah menyelesaikan 12 bulan masa kerja berturut-turut berhak atas 12 hari kerja cuti tahunan berbayar per tahun, sesuai UU Ketenagakerjaan (UU Cipta Kerja). Maksimal 6 hari sisa cuti dapat dibawa ke tahun berikutnya; sisanya hangus pada 31 Desember. Ajukan cuti tahunan melalui portal HR minimal 3 hari kerja sebelumnya.

## 2. Sick Leave | Cuti Sakit
Paid sick leave is granted with a doctor's certificate (surat keterangan dokter) for absences of 2 consecutive days or more. Wages during prolonged illness are paid according to the Manpower Law schedule: 100% for the first 4 months, then a decreasing percentage.
---
Cuti sakit berbayar diberikan dengan surat keterangan dokter untuk ketidakhadiran 2 hari berturut-turut atau lebih. Upah selama sakit berkepanjangan dibayar sesuai ketentuan UU Ketenagakerjaan: 100% untuk 4 bulan pertama, kemudian menurun secara bertahap.

## 3. Maternity & Parental Leave | Cuti Melahirkan & Keluarga
Female employees are entitled to 3 months of paid maternity leave (1.5 months before and 1.5 months after birth) and 1.5 months of paid leave in case of miscarriage, per the Manpower Law. Fathers receive 2 days of paid paternity leave. Female employees may also take 2 days of menstrual leave (cuti haid) on the first and second day of menstruation.
---
Karyawati berhak atas 3 bulan cuti melahirkan berbayar (1,5 bulan sebelum dan 1,5 bulan sesudah melahirkan) serta 1,5 bulan cuti berbayar bila mengalami keguguran, sesuai UU Ketenagakerjaan. Ayah mendapatkan 2 hari cuti berbayar untuk kelahiran. Karyawati juga dapat mengambil cuti haid 2 hari pada hari pertama dan kedua menstruasi.

## 4. Special Paid Leave | Cuti Penting
Employees receive paid leave for important life events under the Manpower Law: the employee's own marriage (3 days), a child's marriage (2 days), the circumcision or baptism of a child (2 days), the birth of a child (2 days), and the death of a spouse, parent, child, or in-law (2 days).
---
Karyawan mendapat cuti berbayar untuk peristiwa penting sesuai UU Ketenagakerjaan: pernikahan karyawan sendiri (3 hari), pernikahan anak (2 hari), khitanan atau baptis anak (2 hari), kelahiran anak (2 hari), serta kematian suami/istri, orang tua, anak, atau mertua (2 hari).
